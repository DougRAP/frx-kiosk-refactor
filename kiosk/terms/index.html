  2. 2. The date of that payment is Day 1;
  3. 3. Your first Coverage Term begins on Day 31 and continues for thirty (30) days;

Example: If Your first Subscription payment is made on January 1, Your first Coverage Term begins on January 31. Your next Subscription payment is due thirty (30) days after the January 1 payment and purchases the Coverage Term that begins immediately after the first Coverage Term expires.

C. PURCHASE WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER DELIVERY

If You purchase the Subscription on or before the thirtieth (30th) day after the Delivery Date:

  1. 1. Your first Subscription payment is due immediately;
  2. 2. The date of that payment is Day 1;
  3. 3. Your first Coverage Term begins on Day 61 and continues for thirty (30) days;
  4. 4. Your second Subscription payment is due on Day 31 and purchases the next Coverage Term, which begins on Day 91; and
  5. 5. Each subsequent Subscription payment is due every thirty (30) days and purchases the next consecutive thirty (30)-day Coverage Term. This means that two Subscription payments must be successfully made before Your first Coverage Term begins.

Example: If You enroll and make Your first Subscription payment on January 1, Your second Subscription payment is due thirty (30) days later. Your first Coverage Term begins on Day 61. The first payment purchases the first Coverage Term, and the second payment purchases the thirty (30)-day Coverage Term that immediately follows it.

D. CONTINUING PAYMENTS AND COVERAGE TERMS

Subscription payments remain due every thirty (30) days, including during any waiting period and while You have active coverage. Each payment purchases the next consecutive thirty (30)-day Coverage Term. A payment does not purchase immediate or retroactive coverage and cannot be applied to damage that occurred before the Coverage Term purchased by that payment begins. We may attempt to charge Your payment method up to four (4) days before each scheduled payment due date. If the payment is successfully collected early, the payment still applies to its regularly scheduled future Coverage Term and does not cause that Coverage Term to begin early.

To maintain continuous coverage, each Subscription payment must be successfully made by its scheduled payment due date.

E. NO COVERAGE DURING A WAITING PERIOD

No coverage is provided before Your first Coverage Term begins. Damage, stains, breakdowns, or other events that occur before the first Coverage Term begins are not covered, even if the damage or event is not discovered or reported until after coverage begins. This Plan does not provide retroactive coverage. A payment made after coverage has lapsed does not restore coverage for any earlier period.

F. IDENTIFICATION AND ALLOCATION OF COVERED ITEMS

Your Subscription applies only to the Items identified or allocated for coverage under the Subscription, subject to the applicable Retail Limit and all other terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of this Plan. You may identify the Items to be covered by uploading the applicable Sales Order and selecting or otherwise designating the covered Items.

If a Sales Order is uploaded when You purchase the Subscription or when You file a Claim, but no specific Items have been identified for coverage, We will allocate coverage in the order in which the Items appear on the Sales Order. Beginning with the first Item listed, We will apply the Retail Limit to the retail price of each Item until the Retail Limit is exhausted. If the remaining Retail Limit is less than the retail price of the next Item listed, that Item will be covered only up to the remaining Retail Limit. Items appearing later on the Sales Order will not be covered after the Retail Limit has been exhausted. Once coverage has been allocated to the Items on a Sales Order, that allocation cannot be reordered, transferred, or changed to obtain coverage for a different Item or increase the coverage available for another Item.

Example: If the Sales Order lists Items with retail prices of $2,000, $2,000, and $2,000, in that order, and the Subscription has a $5,000 Retail Limit, the first and second Items will be included at their full retail prices. The third Item will be included only up to the remaining $1,000 Retail Limit.
