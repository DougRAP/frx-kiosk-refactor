How to use

  • Open any wizard, pick the environment (Live is the default), type your name, and start.
  • For each case: do the steps, press Pass / Fail / N/A, and leave a note. Each section also has a free "General observations" box.
  • Progress is saved automatically in that browser. Use Export progress often to get a JSON backup, and Import to restore it or continue on another machine.
  • The four wizards keep separate storage, so different people can run different fronts without clashing.
